# Invite Contest

Settings ▸ 🏆 Invite Contest (on by default). A ranking of who brings the most people into the group.

| Command | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| `/mylink` | everyone | personal invite link; everybody joining through it counts for you |
| `/myinvites` | everyone | your rank and points + the first ranking page |
| `/topinvites` | everyone | ranking, **10 per page**, ◀️ Prev / Next ▶️ |
| `/invitepoints @user +3` / `-2` | admins | add or remove points (reply works too) |
| `/invitereset` | founder | reset the contest |

* People added by hand with Telegram's *Add members* count for whoever added them.
* A person counts once. With **Leavers lose the point** on, someone who leaves is subtracted again.
* The same actions exist in Settings ▸ Invite Contest (Ranking, My link, Points, Reset).
* The bot needs the **Invite users** admin right to create personal links.
